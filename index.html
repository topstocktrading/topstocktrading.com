FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

Do I need any experience to start? +
Absolutely not. All of our courses are designed for complete beginners. We start from what the stock market is and build from there.
How do the SMS alerts work? +
Once you enroll in an alerts plan, you'll receive a text message directly to your phone whenever there's a trade opportunity. Each alert includes the stock ticker, entry price, target price, stop loss, and a brief explanation of why the trade is being made.
How much money do I need to start trading? +
You can start with as little as $100 with most modern brokerages that offer fractional shares. We recommend starting small while you're learning and scaling up as your confidence grows.
Is trading guaranteed to make money? +
No – and anyone who tells you otherwise is lying. Trading involves real risk and you can lose money. Our goal is to educate you so you can make informed decisions and manage your risk properly.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +
Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time with no penalty. You'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.
