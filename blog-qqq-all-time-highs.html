What Happened

The Nasdaq 100 broke to all-time highs on Tuesday, posting its second consecutive record close and gaining 0.81% on the session. The S&P 500 was flat while the Dow dipped — a clear rotation story where money is moving out of value and defensive names and into growth and technology.

The catalyst: Meta's AI agent "Muse" launched to strong early demand, sending META surging nearly 12% on Monday and carrying the momentum into Tuesday. That single stock move was enough to reignite the entire AI trade and pull the broader tech complex higher.

The AI Trade Is Back On

After a period of consolidation and sideways action, AI stocks are leading again. AMD crossed the $1 trillion market cap threshold — joining NVIDIA and Microsoft in a club that barely existed five years ago. Semiconductor stocks logged their sixth consecutive winning session, their longest streak since April. The underlying thesis hasn't changed: AI infrastructure spending is accelerating, data center demand is at record levels, and the companies building the picks and shovels of this technology cycle are printing money.

The Macro Context

Note the divergence: the Dow dropped 0.35% while QQQ hit all-time highs. This is a rotation, not a broad market rally. Money is concentrated in specific names and themes. If you're not in the right sectors, you're not participating.

What All-Time Highs Mean Technically

When an index breaks to all-time highs, there is no overhead resistance. There are no trapped buyers from previous peaks looking to break even and sell. The only thing above is open air, which is why breakouts to new highs often produce faster and larger moves than rallies back into prior trading ranges.

That said, breakouts at all-time highs also carry more risk than breakouts in the middle of a range. There's no natural support above — which means if the breakout fails and the index reverses below the prior high, the move down can be sharp.

Key Levels to Watch

  • QQQ above $743 — confirmed breakout trigger, first ATH target at $747 and $750
  • Support at $738 — the prior all-time high now becomes support if the market pulls back
  • NVDA at $138 — the key level for the semiconductor story. Holds above = AI trade intact
  • 10-Year yield at 4.85% — a rapid move toward 5% would put pressure on growth valuations
