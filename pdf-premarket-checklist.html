Pre-Market Prep Checklist
What to do before the market opens every single day
The best traders are prepared before the bell rings. This checklist takes 20–30 minutes and gives you a clear plan before you risk a single dollar. Traders who skip pre-market prep are reactive. Traders who complete it are prepared.
1Market Context (5 min)
  • Check futures — are they up, down, or flat? By how much?
  • Check the VIX — is volatility elevated or compressed?
  • Any major economic events today? (Fed speak, CPI, earnings?)
  • What did the major indexes do yesterday? Any key levels to watch?
  • Is the overall market in an uptrend, downtrend, or choppy?
2Build Your Watchlist (10 min)
  • Run your scanner for stocks with pre-market volume and news
  • Check for earnings movers — any big beats or misses pre-market?
  • Look for stocks at key technical levels — breakout candidates
  • Identify 3–5 stocks maximum. Quality over quantity.
  • For each stock: note the key level, the setup, and your plan
3Set Your Levels (5 min)
  • Mark pre-market high and low on your chart for each watchlist stock
  • Mark prior day high and low
  • Mark any obvious support or resistance levels
  • Know the opening range setup for each stock if applicable
4Risk Check (2 min)
  • What is your max loss for today?
  • Am I in a good mental state to trade? (Tired, stressed, angry = no trading)
  • Do I have a plan for each trade, or am I just going to react?
TST Rule
If you cannot complete this checklist, do not trade that day. A prepared trader with a plan beats an unprepared trader every time — over thousands of trades.
