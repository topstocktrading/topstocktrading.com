Chart Patterns Cheat Sheet
The most important patterns every active trader must recognize
Chart patterns are visual formations created by price action that signal a likely continuation or reversal of the current trend. Knowing these patterns before they complete — not after — is what separates prepared traders from reactive ones.

3Key Rules
Volume confirms the pattern
A breakout on low volume is suspect. A breakout on high volume (relative to average) is a real signal. Always check volume before entering a pattern trade.
Wait for the break, not the anticipation
Many beginners enter before the pattern completes. The pattern is only valid once price breaks the key level. Anticipating costs you money in failed patterns.
⚠ Fake breakouts are common
Price breaks a key level, triggers entries, then reverses. Use a small buffer above/below the breakout level for entry and always have a defined stop.
