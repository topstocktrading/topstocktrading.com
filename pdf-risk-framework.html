TST Risk Framework
The rules every trader needs before placing a single trade
Most traders lose money not because they can't read charts — but because they have no rules around risk. This framework gives you a simple, repeatable system to protect your capital on every single trade.
1The 1% Rule
Maximum Risk Per Trade
Risk = Account Size × 0.01
$10,000 account → max $100 risk per trade
Why it matters
Risking 1% means you can lose 20 trades in a row and still have 80% of your capital. Most beginners risk 10–20% per trade and blow up their account in days.
2Position Sizing
Shares to Buy
Shares = Dollar Risk ÷ (Entry − Stop)
$100 risk, entry $50, stop $48 → buy 50 shares
Always calculate before entry
Know your share size before you click buy. Position sizing is not optional — it is the difference between a manageable loss and an account-ending one.

4Daily Loss Limit
Hard Rule
Set a maximum daily loss — typically 2–3% of your account. When you hit it, you are done for the day. No exceptions. The market opens tomorrow.
⚠ The biggest mistake beginners make
Trying to "make back" losses in the same session. This is revenge trading. It leads to bigger position sizes, worse decisions, and deeper losses. Your daily loss limit exists to stop this before it starts.
5The Pre-Trade Checklist
  • I know exactly where my stop is before I enter
  • I have calculated my position size using the formula above
  • My risk on this trade is 1% or less of my account
  • My reward is at least 2x my risk (2:1 R:R minimum)
  • I am not at my daily loss limit
  • I am not trading out of emotion or trying to make back losses
