Daily Watchlist Builder
Step-by-step guide to building your trading watchlist every morning
A quality watchlist is built the night before or early morning. The goal is not to find every moving stock — it is to find 3–5 high-probability setups with a clear plan.
1Start With the Market
Index bias first
Check S&P 500 and Nasdaq direction before scanning. Trading longs in a down market is fighting the tape. Know your macro context first.
2Scanner Criteria
FilterSettingWhy
Price$2 – $500Avoids penny stocks and no-liquidity names
Volume500k+ avg/dayYou need to enter and exit easily
Relative Volume1.5x or higherIncreased interest — something is happening
FloatUnder 50M (momentum)Low float moves faster on volume spikes
CatalystFilter by newsVolume without a reason is noise
3Rate Each Stock (1–5)
  • Is there a clear catalyst driving the move?
  • Is the chart at a logical technical level (not extended)?
  • Is volume significantly above average?
  • Is the R:R at least 2:1?
  • Does this fit one of my defined setup types?
Only trade 4s and 5s
Stocks scoring 3 or below stay on the bench. Most losing trades come from forcing mediocre setups.
