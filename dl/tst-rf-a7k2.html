TST Risk Framework
Complete guide to protecting your account before you place a single trade
Most traders lose money not because they can't read charts — they fail because they have no rules around risk. This framework gives you a repeatable system to protect your capital on every trade.
1The 1% Rule
Max Risk Per Trade
Risk = Account Size × 0.01
$10,000 account → max $100 risk per trade
Why it matters
Risk 1% and you survive 20 losing trades with 80% of your capital intact. Most beginners risk 10–20% and blow up in days.
2Position Sizing
Shares to Buy
Shares = Dollar Risk ÷ (Entry − Stop)
$100 risk · entry $50 · stop $48 → buy 50 shares

4Daily Loss Limit
Hard Rule
Set a daily max loss — 2–3% of account. Hit it and stop trading. No exceptions. The market opens tomorrow.
⚠ Revenge Trading
Never try to "make back" losses in the same session. Your daily loss limit exists to stop this before it starts.
5Pre-Trade Checklist
  • I know exactly where my stop is before I enter
  • My position size is calculated — not guessed
  • My risk is 1% or less of my account
  • My reward is at least 2x my risk
  • I have not hit my daily loss limit
  • I am not trading out of emotion
