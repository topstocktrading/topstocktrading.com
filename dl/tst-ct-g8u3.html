Candlesticks & Market Terminology
The visual language of the market with a complete glossary of essential trading terms
The stock market has its own language. Before you can understand what a chart is telling you, you need to know the vocabulary.
1Key Candlestick Types
2Wick Rules
Long upper wick at resistance
Sellers rejected the high aggressively. Bearish — price tried to break out but failed.
Long lower wick at support
Buyers rejected the low aggressively. Bullish — the dip was bought strongly.
3Essential Trading Terms
TermDefinition
FloatShares available for public trading. Low float = fast moves on volume.
RVOLRelative volume vs normal. 2.0 = twice the usual volume — something is happening.
VWAPVolume Weighted Average Price. Key intraday support/resistance level.
CatalystNews event driving the move. Volume without catalyst is noise.
Short SqueezeHeavy short stock rises fast, forcing shorts to buy — driving price even higher.
Level 2Real-time display of all buy/sell orders. Shows where buyers/sellers are stacked.
Gap Up/DownOpens significantly above/below prior close. Direction sets day's bias.
PDT RuleRequires $25k for 4+ day trades in 5 days in a margin account.
SpreadDifference between bid and ask. Wide spread = higher cost to trade.
Circuit BreakerTemporary halt when stock moves too fast. Can resume up or down.
