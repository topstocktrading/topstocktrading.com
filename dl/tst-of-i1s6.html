Options & Futures Basics
Calls, puts, strike prices, ES, NQ — everything explained in plain English
Options and futures are powerful tools most traders don't fully understand before using them. No math formulas. No jargon. Just what you actually need to know.
1Options Basics
TermPlain English
Call OptionRight to BUY 100 shares at the strike price. Buy calls when bullish.
Put OptionRight to SELL 100 shares at the strike price. Buy puts when bearish.
Strike PriceThe price at which you can exercise the option.
ExpirationThe date the option expires. Options lose value as expiration approaches.
PremiumThe price you pay for the contract. Maximum you can lose if wrong.
In The Money (ITM)Stock has passed the strike price in your profitable direction.
Out of The Money (OTM)Stock has not reached strike yet. Cheaper but lower probability.
2Futures Basics
TermPlain English
ES (S&P Futures)Tracks the S&P 500. Each point = $50. Trade overall market direction.
NQ (Nasdaq Futures)Tracks Nasdaq 100. Each point = $20. Tech-heavy index.
MES / MNQMicro versions — 1/10th the size. Better for beginners learning futures.
ContangoFutures price higher than spot. Normal market condition.
BackwardationFutures price lower than spot. Signals unusual demand for immediate delivery.
⚠ Do not trade options or futures until
You have a consistent edge trading stocks. Options amplify both gains and losses. Futures move fast with high margin requirements. Learn to walk before you run.
