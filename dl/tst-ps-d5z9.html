Trading Psychology Cheat Sheet
The 10 most common behavioral mistakes and exactly how to fix each one
Trading is 50% technical skill and 50% mental discipline. Most traders who fail have adequate technical knowledge — they fail because they cannot control their behavior under pressure.
1. Revenge Trading
Taking impulsive trades to make back losses. Fix: hard daily loss limit. When hit, close platform and walk away.
2. FOMO
Chasing moves that already happened. Fix: if you missed the entry, there will be another. Never chase.
3. Overtrading
Trading from boredom. Fix: define max trades per day. Only trade A+ setups.
4. Moving Your Stop
Widening stops to avoid a loss. Fix: your stop is a hard rule, not a suggestion.
5. Cutting Winners Early
Taking profit too soon. Fix: scale out at first target, hold rest to second.
6. Overconfidence
Win streak makes you feel invincible. Fix: rules don't change after wins. Track process, not feelings.
7. Loss Aversion
Holding losers hoping they come back. Fix: accept losses as business cost. Cut and move on.
8. Anchoring
Decisions based on your entry price. Fix: the market doesn't know your entry. Only current price matters.
9. Confirmation Bias
Ignoring signals that contradict your thesis. Fix: actively look for reasons NOT to take the trade.
10. Trading Emotional
Tired, stressed, or angry decisions cost money. Fix: only trade when calm and focused.
⚠ The most expensive statement in trading
"I'll just take this one more trade." Every large account blowup starts with breaking a rule "just once." Your rules exist for the moment when you most want to break them.
