The Trader's Pattern Playbook
The 6 chart patterns that appear most often with visual examples and exact entry rules
These 6 patterns appear constantly in active markets. Mastering them — and knowing exactly where to enter — separates traders with edge from traders who are guessing.
1Bull Flag — Bullish Continuation
Entry
Breakout above the flag on volume. Stop below the flag low. Target = flagpole length projected up.
2Bear Flag — Bearish Continuation
Entry
Breakdown below the flag on volume. Stop above the flag high. Target = flagpole length projected down.
3Head & Shoulders — Bearish Reversal
Entry
Short on break below neckline. Stop above right shoulder. Target = head-to-neckline distance projected down.
4Ascending Triangle — Bullish Breakout
Entry
Long on break above flat resistance on volume. Stop below last higher low.
5Double Top — Bearish Reversal
Entry
Short on break below the valley between the two peaks. Stop above the second peak.
6Cup & Handle — Bullish Continuation
Entry
Long on breakout above handle resistance. Stop below handle low. More common on daily charts.
