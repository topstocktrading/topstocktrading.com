Beginner's Guide to Reading Charts
Candlesticks, support, resistance, volume, and trend — explained without jargon
Before you can trade you must be able to read a chart. A chart is a visual record of every price and volume transaction over time. Everything else builds on this foundation.
1The Axes
Y-axis = Price
Higher on the chart = higher price. The scale on the right shows exact dollar values.
X-axis = Time
Moving left to right = moving forward in time. Each candle = one time period (1 min, 5 min, 1 day, etc.).
2Candlestick Types
CandleWhat it means
GreenClose above open. Buyers won the period.
RedClose below open. Sellers won the period.
Long upper wickSellers rejected the high aggressively.
Long lower wickBuyers rejected the low aggressively.
Large body, no wicksMaximum conviction in one direction.
3Volume
Volume confirms price moves
A breakout on high volume is real. A breakout on low volume is suspect. Always check the volume panel before acting.
4Trend Identification
TrendDefinitionTrade
UptrendHigher highs, higher lowsLook for longs
DowntrendLower highs, lower lowsLook for shorts
ChoppyNo clear directionReduce size or sit out
